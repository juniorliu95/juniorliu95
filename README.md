### About

I build **WalkMe's next-generation computer-using agent at SAP** with the R&D team in Tel Aviv. Before that I spent three years as a **Customer Engineer at Scale AI**, leading proofs of concept and enterprise deployments in Japan, and worked as an **AI researcher at Toshiba**.

My research asks how far large language models can be trusted in medicine, particularly in Japanese. I build safety and clinical-knowledge benchmarks and LLM agents for public-health communication. My doctoral work was in Medical Informatics at Kyoto University.
